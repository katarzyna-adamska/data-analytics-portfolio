Sales Analysis with Spreadsheets

Project Overview

This beginner data analysis project focuses on analyzing product sales, profitability, and profit margins using OpenOffice Calc.

The goal was to organize sales data, calculate key business metrics, compare product performance, and draw conclusions that could support business decisions.

Tools Used

OpenOffice Calc

Spreadsheet formulas

Data sorting and filtering

Data visualization (bar chart)

Analysis Performed

The analysis included:

Calculating total sales revenue for each product

Calculating the cost of sold products

Calculating total profit and profit margin

Comparing product profitability

Evaluating sales performance

Creating a bar chart to compare total profit by product

Preparing business recommendations based on the results

Key Findings

Highest total profit: Lacquer generated the highest total profit of 378 PLN, with 42 units sold.

Lowest profit margin: Mask had the lowest profit margin at 30.8%, while its total profit was 210 PLN. This shows that a lower margin does not necessarily mean the lowest total profit.

Sales scenario: If 50 units of lacquer were sold while maintaining a profit of 9 PLN per unit, total profit would reach 450 PLN.

The sales scenario is a calculation based on an assumed constant profit per unit, not a guaranteed forecast.

Business Recommendation

Based on the analysis, I recommend exploring ways to increase lacquer sales while maintaining the current price.

Lacquer generated the highest total profit in the analyzed product group, reaching 378 PLN from 42 units sold.

A price increase should only be considered after examining customer demand and competitors' prices. The impact of a price change on sales volume and total profit cannot be determined from the available data alone.
